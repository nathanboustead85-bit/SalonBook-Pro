# Marketing consent preview
Explicit channel-specific opt-in only, separate from transactional reminders. Missing, stale, withdrawn or mismatched consent fails closed; suppression and disabled business controls override consent. Pure offline eligibility only. Caller snapshots, recipient ownership, consent provenance and suppression are not authoritative. Production requires verified customer identity, durable consent evidence and withdrawal, atomic server checks before sending, unsubscribe, UK GDPR/PECR review, provider approval, abuse and cost controls, retention and audit. No marketing delivery or live deployment.
