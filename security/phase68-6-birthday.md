# Birthday reward preview
Optional month/day only, no birth year. UK local calendar, Feb 29 maps to Feb 28 in non-leap years. Validity 1–90 calendar days starting birthday inclusive, expiry exclusive; December windows can extend into January and claim year is birthday year. One proposal per customer per birthday year against matching ledger snapshots, explicit channel marketing consent and suppression checks. Discount calculated from supplied service and price. No birthday storage, scheduling, sending, or actual reward issuance. Production requires authoritative identity, consent, birthday edits and audit, immutable annual claim ledger with atomic concurrency protection, trusted clock, offer and booking binding, redemption expiry enforcement, privacy retention/deletion and customer UI. No deployment.
