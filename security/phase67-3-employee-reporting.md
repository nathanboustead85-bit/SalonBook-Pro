# Employee reporting foundations
Owner-only offline grouping by existing appointment staff string; authoritative immutable staff IDs and historical attribution are required before production (names may collide or change). Average is per completed appointment, not per distinct customer. Contribution is completed appointment value divided by team completed value; rounded shares may not sum to 100%. Zero denominators are unavailable. Employees without in-range records are absent. No payment receipts, cash/card split, tips, discounts, refunds, commission or chair rental settlement yet; reconcile against authoritative payment ledger later. Own-only employee reports require new explicit permission and backend enforcement. No UI, live access or deployment.
