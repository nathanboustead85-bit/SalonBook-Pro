# Service reporting foundations
Offline owner-only reporting grouped by existing service string; immutable service IDs and historical attribution required before production. Popularity sorted by completed appointment count; services without in-range records absent, so zero-demand catalogue services require catalogue integration. Cancellation rate is canceled records divided by all in-range records, including upcoming bookings; not a final cohort cancellation rate. Average and contribution use completed appointment values, not receipts or profit. Rounded percentages may not sum to 100. Zero denominators unavailable. Authoritative identity, service catalogue, payment ledger, UI and backend integration remain pending. No live deployment.
