# Employee access and cross-platform support

Approved roadmap addition: 25 November 2026. Mandatory before commercial launch.

## Phase 66 — employee access
Individual staff accounts, invitations, owner/manager/employee roles, configurable least-privilege permissions, own versus all appointments, restricted customer contact and messaging access, portfolio and availability controls, financial and subscription restrictions, immediate revocation and audit logs. Enforce permissions on the backend, including tenant isolation and revoked sessions. Default deny. Test direct API access and cross-business access.

## Phase 70 — PWA
Responsive installable business dashboard for iPhone, iPad, Android and desktop. Safari Add to Home Screen, secure authentication, restricted staff dashboard, safe cache/logout behavior, accessibility and notification capability testing. Native iOS app not required initially.

## Integration and launch gates
Cross-device synchronization and conflict protection, real iPhone/iPad testing, employee invitation/revocation and permission regression tests, privacy and operational reliability. No live feature activation or deployment without existing approvals. Preserve original DEV appointments and client records. Estimate 23 overlapping work sections; incremental effort and schedule subject to review. Employee accounts included in affordable subscription is a pricing preference subject to sustainable costs.
